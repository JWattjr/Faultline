# Faultline reviewer desk

## Mode

Operate

## Mechanism

One immutable charter binds a three-agent handoff trace to independent GenLayer adjudication and deterministic DEMO settlement.

## Audience scene

A reviewer opens the app without a wallet and needs to trace one charter from source URLs through Research, Analysis, and Delivery, then distinguish validator interpretation, consensus finality, execution, and the receipt. A wallet is needed only to change contract state.

## Direction contract

- **World:** An original playful filing-desk world: sky-blue outside, a cream paper docket with a thick irregular graphite edge, folder tabs, dashed dividers, capsule status labels, and three original SVG agent mascots. Handwritten lowercase headings pair with IBM Plex Mono throughout. Evidence and state labels remain the focus; no franchise art, gradient, glass, or generic crypto dashboard.
- **First viewport:** The product purpose and live Studio Next status sit above a selected charter docket. The docket shows its state, escrow and bond summary, three handoffs with edge checks, a current/expected outcome with its live-vs-fixture label, and a direct route to source evidence.
- **Visitor path:** Choose a charter, follow each handoff and its hash, inspect the frozen clauses and evidence, separate consensus/finality from execution, then read the contract-calculated settlement or open retry.
- **Signature interaction:** Selecting another docket swaps the complete trace while retaining an explicit badge that says whether its contents came from contract reads or synthetic local fixtures. A Plain numbers switch replaces decorative marks and responsibility bars with text and a table.
- **Cross-surface reach:** Use the same evidence marks and vocabulary in contract method controls, public receipts, fixture pages, and submission docs.
- **Honest risk:** The deployment may be absent or Studio Next may be unavailable. In either case show an explicit unavailable state and a separately labeled synthetic preview; never present preview values as live.

## Code-led choice

The user chose code first and the brief pins the dossier visual system and first-viewport contents. The implemented structure is a case register beside one active docket, followed by a five-part evidence-to-settlement reading rail. This keeps the five required layers visible without turning them into five dashboard metric cards.

## Quality bar

- Every transaction status distinguishes pending, finalized-success, and finalized-error execution.
- Every source or handoff hash is shown as contract data or as a labeled fixture digest.
- An HTTP 200 evidence/artifact hash mismatch is labeled as the deterministic `EVIDENCE_TAMPERED` basis and shows both full digests; it remains distinct from validator judgment.
- Preview outcomes and accounting calculations are labeled expected/synthetic, and no preview value appears as on-chain state.
- The controls work by keyboard, remain legible on mobile, and never require a wallet just to inspect proof.
